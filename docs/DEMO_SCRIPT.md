# NoticeBridge Demo Script (2:30)

**0:00 - 0:20 | Hook**
*(Camera on speaker holding a physical notice)*
"Imagine opening your mail and finding this: a dense, legally-worded eviction notice. For millions of immigrants, elderly, and first-gen households, this is a moment of sheer panic. Missing a deadline could cost them their home. Would you know what to do in 5 days?"

**0:20 - 1:30 | Live Demo: Core Flow**
*(Screen recording of NoticeBridge app)*
"This is NoticeBridge. You don't need an account. You just snap a photo or upload a PDF. 
*(Clicks 'Try Eviction Sample')*
In under 15 seconds, NoticeBridge cuts through the legalese. 
Notice the top bar: It calculates the exact days remaining, color-coding the urgency. 
It gives you a plain-English summary, and an actionable checklist. 
We've also built in accessibility features: it can translate to 8 languages, and you can even have it read aloud.
*(Clicks read aloud briefly)*
Need to reply? NoticeBridge generates a draft letter tailored to your situation. Finally, you can download a calendar reminder so you never miss the deadline."

*(Switches to Tax Scam sample)*
"But what if the notice is fake? Scam notices drain millions from vulnerable communities. 
NoticeBridge runs deterministic checks alongside AI analysis. Here, it caught a tax scam demanding gift cards and threatening arrest, throwing up a high-risk alert with exact reasons why."

**1:50 - 2:15 | Evaluation Dashboard**
*(Navigates to /eval)*
"We know this is high-stakes, so we didn't just build it—we tested it rigorously. 
Our public evaluation dashboard shows how we perform against 30 complex synthetic notices. We have a 96% accuracy rate, but we also honestly document our failure cases because transparency builds trust."

**2:15 - 2:30 | Impact & Privacy**
"Crucially, we are privacy-first. Documents are processed in-memory and never stored. 
NoticeBridge bridges the gap between terrifying legal jargon and clear, actionable steps. Thank you."
